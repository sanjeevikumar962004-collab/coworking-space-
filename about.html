Our purpose

To give good ideas a place to grow, and the people behind them a place to belong.

Independent minds shouldn't have to choose between a lonely kitchen table and the noise of a café. Growing teams shouldn't drown in the headaches of managing an office.

Stackly exists to create that balance: space to bring your quiet focus and find your people when you need them. Right here in Salem.

What we believe in

Considered in every sense.

01

Designed for focus

Natural light, acoustic comfort, and clutter-free environments so you can do deep work without distraction.

02

Flexible by intention

From hour-long drop-ins to team offices, our spaces move and scale around your day, never the other way around.

03

Hospitality at heart

A warm welcome, fresh brew, and every detail taken care of, so you can focus on what matters.

Designed for real work

For the work you do. And what comes next.

The spaces between the work

Our spaces foster organic community—never forced networking, just real people doing inspiring work side by side.
